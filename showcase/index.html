Showcase

All demos below use sample data from British modernist literature — no corpus upload required.

Semantic Space

The Semantic 3D View positions every work in your corpus by meaning. Works are embedded using a sentence-transformer model, reduced to three dimensions with UMAP, and rendered as an interactive point cloud. Similar texts naturally cluster together — novels by the same author will often form tight constellations, while essays and poetry drift to the periphery.

Temporal Patterns

The Temporal View exposes how your corpus is distributed across time through three linked charts. Switch between a publication timeline, a stacked streamgraph by genre, and a scatter plot of year versus a chosen metric.

Geospatial Distribution

The Geospatial View maps place references extracted from your texts by named entity recognition. Markers are sized by mention count, so the geography of your corpus becomes immediately legible — which cities dominate? Which continents are absent?

Close Reading

The Close Reading View is where textual analysis happens at the passage level. Open any document to access annotations, semantic search, and NLP metrics side-by-side with the text.

Annotations

Colour-coded reader annotations pinned to specific passages. Each annotation carries a label colour, a short note, and a timestamp. Click a card in the tray to jump to the corresponding span in the text.

Semantic search

Live BM25-style passage retrieval over the corpus. Type a query and all passages are reranked by relevance in real time, with matching terms highlighted directly in each result snippet.

NLP analysis

Per-document NLP output: named entity type counts (persons, places, organisations), a passage-level sentiment score, and a narrative arc chart showing how emotional register shifts across the fourteen sections of the work.

Corpus sentiment

Corpus-level sentiment analysis aggregated across all loaded works. The diverging bar chart makes the emotional register of each work immediately comparable. Switch between individual works and per-author averages to move between micro and macro perspectives.

Passage similarity

Given any passage as a query, retrieves the five most semantically similar passages from the rest of the corpus. Hover a result card to expand the full passage text.
